Apart – Privacy Policy

Apart – Magnet Puzzle, by Raqlay Labs (Naman Jain). Effective 14 October 2026.

Apart is a puzzle game. You don't create an account, and the game doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (which board you are on) and your settings (sound and vibration) are saved on your phone so the game can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report so we can fix the problem. We use Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) to receive these reports. A report contains technical details only:

  • what went wrong in the app's code (an error message and a stack trace);
  • the app version, the phone model, the Android version, and basic device state such as free memory;
  • a random identifier for this installation of the app, used to count crash-free sessions.

Crash reports don't include your name, email, contacts, location or what's on your screen. We have set Sentry not to store IP addresses. Reports are sent over an encrypted connection and kept for up to 90 days.

Sharing

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising. Crash reports are processed by Sentry only to provide the service to us.

Children

Apart is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any crash reports linked to your installation by emailing us. Because the reports don't contain your name or email, tell us roughly when the crash happened and your phone model so we can find them.

Changes

If the app starts collecting anything new (for example, ads or anonymous play statistics), we will update this page before that version is released and change the date above.
